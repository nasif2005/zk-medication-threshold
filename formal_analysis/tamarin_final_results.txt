Interpretation
--------------
Under the modeled ideal randomized zero-knowledge-proof abstraction, Tamarin
verified non-derivability of the private medication history, active-window
aggregate, and state randomness in both privacy worlds. It also verified
executability of proof publication and MEA verification and confirmed that an
accepted proof originated from the modeled publication rule.

The equivalence theory verified that two distinct private witnesses are
observationally equivalent when they expose the same modeled public request,
policy, time, Merkle root, nullifier, successor commitment, and authorization
outcome.


Scope and limitations
---------------------
- The equivalence result is conditional on identical permitted public outputs.
- The equivalence experiment models one request.
- The zk-SNARK is represented as an ideal opaque randomized proof constructor.
- The analysis does not prove Groth16 zero knowledge or soundness, Circom
  circuit correctness, rolling-window arithmetic, Merkle correctness,
  commitment security, implementation security, adaptive multi-request
  privacy, or resistance to timing and other side channels.
- Privacy across different public nullifiers, commitments, policy contexts, or
  authorization outcomes is not claimed.


Commands used
-------------
Trace lemmas were proved individually with commands of the form:

  tamarin-prover --diff --prove=<lemma_name> medication_threshold_privacy.spthy

The clean equivalence proof used:

  tamarin-prover --diff --prove medication_threshold_equivalence.spthy
